Historical directions only

These directions were published for an old event at Centennial Ranch and should not be used for the current Vera Family Reunion.

Archived address: Golden Gate Canyon Road, Golden, Colorado 80403-8000.

The original directions instructed guests to travel west of Denver to the junction of Highways 6, 58, and 93 on the west side of Golden; proceed north on Highway 93 for approximately one mile; turn left onto Golden Gate Canyon Road; and continue approximately nine miles to Centennial Ranch.
