Sunday

Traditional Peruvian Menu

A traditional Peruvian Sunday feast inspired by the flavors of the Andes, the coast, and classic family gatherings.

Main Entrées

  • Anticucho-Style Roast Meats Slow-roasted pork shoulder marinated in a smoky, savory glaze of ají panca, garlic, red wine vinegar, cumin, and oregano.
  • Pachamanca-Style Roast Meats Slow-roasted pork shoulder and beef infused with traditional Andean herbs, fragrant huacatay, garlic, beer, and lime.

Vegetarian Feature

  • Papas a la Huancaína Tender sliced yellow potatoes served chilled or at room temperature, covered in a velvety ají amarillo and queso fresco sauce, and garnished with quartered hard-boiled eggs and Peruvian botija olives.

Starches & Warm Sides

  • Arroz con Ajo Classic Peruvian long-grain white rice gently simmered with garlic and olive oil.
  • Plátanos Maduros Asados Baked sweet ripe plantains, caramelized and served warm.
  • Habas al Huacatay Tender boiled broad beans (fava beans) tossed with olive oil, garlic, and aromatic Andean black mint (huacatay).
  • Grilled Corn & Summer Vegetables Sweet corn on the cob, sliced bell peppers, and mushrooms braised and charred over the grill.
  • Papas y Camotes Sliced boiled yellow potatoes and sweet potatoes served warm.
  • Cauliflower Rice Savory seasoned cauliflower rice for a lighter, vegan option.

Salads & Relishes

  • Ensalada Fresca Crisp chopped romaine lettuce, sliced cucumbers, and radishes topped with sliced avocado and a bright lime–olive oil vinaigrette.
  • Sarsa Criolla Classic Peruvian relish of ice-crisped shaved red onions, julienned plum tomatoes, sliced chiles, and fresh cilantro tossed with lime juice and vinegar.

Signature Table Sauce

  • Ají Verde Chilled, creamy Peruvian green sauce blended with fresh cilantro, ají amarillo, garlic, cottage cheese, and lime.

Grazing Snacks

  • Cancha Serrana Pan-toasted crunchy Andean chulpe corn seasoned with sea salt.
  • Chifles Crispy salted plantain chips.

Dessert

  • Humitas Dulces Hand-wrapped steamed sweet corn tamales spiced with cinnamon and anise, with raisins, served warm.

Bar & Beverage Station

  • Signature Cocktails Freshly shaken classic Pisco Sours and refreshing Chilcanos made with Peruvian pisco, ginger ale, fresh lime juice, and bitters.
  • Traditional & Non-Alcoholic Chicha Morada (spiced purple corn punch), Inca Kola, and bottled water.
  • Beer Chilled Coors beer.
Vegetarian and vegan-friendly selections are available throughout the menu.
